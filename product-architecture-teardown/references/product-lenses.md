# Product-category lenses

Use the smallest set of lenses that explains the product. Classification is provisional until evidence supports it.

## AIGC creation product

Prioritize:

- input brief, prompt, script, references, style, ratio, duration, language, and model parameters;
- generation stages and confirmation gates;
- consistency across characters, scenes, objects, shots, voice, and style;
- asset lineage, stable references, versions, preview, editing, composition, export, and publishing;
- model capability limits, cost, latency, safety, quality checks, retries, and partial regeneration;
- whether upstream edits invalidate only the affected downstream assets.

The central question is whether creative intent becomes controllable, reusable assets and a verifiably complete final output.

## General execution Agent

Prioritize:

- goal interpretation, planning, delegation, tool authorization, and progress reporting;
- working context, memory, task state, tool results, and external side effects;
- human approval before high-impact actions;
- idempotency, resumability, rollback, interruption, timeout, and audit trail;
- completion criteria based on external results rather than Agent narration;
- boundaries between planner, specialist Agents, tools, and deterministic policy services.

The central question is whether intent is converted into safe, observable, recoverable execution.

## Conversational companion

Prioritize:

- persona, relationship continuity, conversation memory, personalization, and emotional response;
- session versus long-term memory, consent, edit/delete controls, and privacy boundaries;
- crisis and safety handling, age suitability, dependency risks, escalation, and refusal behavior;
- latency, turn-taking, voice, multimodal expression, notification behavior, and re-engagement;
- what is generated from the current turn, recalled from memory, or inferred about the user.

The central question is whether continuity and emotional value are delivered without hidden memory, unsafe dependency, or privacy loss.

## Workflow or business SaaS

Prioritize:

- roles, permissions, records, forms, approvals, collaboration, search, reporting, import/export, and integrations;
- lifecycle states, ownership, audit logs, concurrency, notifications, and exception handling;
- structured data integrity, tenant isolation, compliance, and operational metrics;
- automation rules versus human decisions.

The central question is whether shared business state moves accurately through accountable decisions.

## Search, knowledge, or research product

Prioritize:

- query formation, source discovery, retrieval, ranking, grounding, citation, freshness, and coverage;
- source permissions, document ingestion, chunking, indexing, knowledge updates, and deletion;
- synthesis versus quotation, conflicting sources, uncertainty, and evaluation;
- private and public knowledge isolation.

The central question is whether answers are traceable, current, complete enough, and permission-safe.

## Hybrid products

Name one primary category, then list secondary lenses and why they matter. Do not merge every checklist. For example, an AIGC product with execution Agents uses the AIGC lens for asset quality and lineage, and the execution-Agent lens for orchestration, approvals, and retries.
